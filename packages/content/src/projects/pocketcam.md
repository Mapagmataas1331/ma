Open PocketCam on Windows 11, scan the QR in Safari or Chrome, and allow the camera. OBS, Discord, Zoom, Teams, and the Windows Camera app see a camera named PocketCam. The stream stays on the local network. Quality runs from 480p to 4K, and a recording keeps the phone’s original H.264.
